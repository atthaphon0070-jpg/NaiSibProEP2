# EP20 beat map

Times in seconds from the start of each narration file. Phrase starts come from a 10 ms RMS silence map (EP20/tools/segment.py). Single-word beats (digits, place values, choices) come from syllable onsets on the 5 ms envelope plus spectrogram reading (EP20/tools/syllables.py, spectro.py); ศูนย์/สอง/สี่/สิบ/สาม were confirmed by their /s/ fricative onset.

Every visual beat in `source/index.html` reads its time from `source/beats.js` (the same numbers as below). "Abs" is the time in the final video and "Frame" is its 30 fps frame index.
